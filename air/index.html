01 · The idea

One feature, eight ways.

The Air Max is the shoe where you can see the air. So the spot never leaves that idea: the Air unit squashes on impact, the camera flies inside it, the word AIR inflates, the shoe sits in a wind tunnel. Eight scenes, one feature.

It is a spec piece built by the Lab with a real, licensed 3D model. The model came in its violet colorway, so the ad turns it into Infrared on screen, in a shader, instead of repainting textures by hand.

00:01 · master prompt · claude code
Build a 24-second vertical spec ad for the Nike Air Max 90 called "Visible Air" in Remotion (React) with three.js through @remotion/three, 1080x1920 at 30 fps. The idea: the Air Max is the shoe where you can see the air, so every scene is about the Air unit. Use a real 3D shoe, never one built from boxes.

Model: load the CC BY 4.0 glTF "Air Max 90" by offshoreclipping with GLTFLoader and the meshopt decoder. Scale it x10 (1 unit = 10 cm), length along X with the heel toward +X, sole on y = 0. Put a rounded glass box (MeshPhysicalMaterial: transmission 1, ior 1.5, dispersion 6, clearcoat) inside the heel window as the Air unit, and make the four pillars inside the window frosted glass.

One vertex patch for every material (onBeforeCompile): take each vertex to shoe space with the inverse of the shoe's world matrix, squash the midsole in the heel (y scaled by 1 - squash * heel weight, the upper drops by the same amount, the sole bulges to the sides), then back to world space. squash > 0 compresses, squash < 0 stretches. Also in every material, a recolor in the fragment shader: read the color after the texture, convert it to HSV, mask the violet accents (hue around 0.70, saturation above 0.2) and mix them to Infrared (#FF3B4A, keeping the brightness) only behind a front that moves along the shoe, with a thin emissive glow on the front itself.

Look: a black-to-deep-red studio drawn as a screen-space gradient, a floor that fades into the background with a radial alpha map (no horizon line), a soft fake contact shadow, environment lighting only (drei Environment with Lightformers, a red rim from behind), ACES at exposure 1.25, dpr 1.5. Type: Anton (OFL) in caps for headlines and Inter for sublines, from local files with FontFace. Close on the official Swoosh, flat and white.

Scenes, one idea per 3 seconds:
1. 0-3s: "VISIBLE AIR." The shoe is already falling at frame 0 and hits the floor at frame 10; the Air unit squashes and springs back with a damped spring and a small second bounce. A shockwave ring on the floor and dust with gravity.
2. 3-6s: "SINCE 1987. The first window of Air." The camera dives into the Air window until the glass fills the frame.
3. 6-9s: "PURE AIR. Pressurized nitrogen." Hard cut inside the Air unit: tall glass columns with dispersion, red and white light bars behind them for the glass to refract, procedural caustics on the floor and bubbles rising.
4. 9-12s: "INFRARED. The original colorway." A vertical sheet of light sweeps from heel to toe and the violet turns Infrared behind it.
5. 12-15s: The word AIR inflates in 3D behind the floating shoe, letter by letter, on a cream set. Each letter is a balloon: draw the glyph on a canvas, compute every pixel's distance to the edge, blur it, give the front a circular profile (zero at the edge, full in the middle), mirror it for the back, cut both with the glyph as an alpha mask, and inflate by scaling z with a spring.
6. 15-18s: "EVERY STEP. Compressed. Returned." Macro of the heel, three slow steps, dust on each.
7. 18-21s: "TINKER HATFIELD. Designed it in 1990." A wind tunnel: streamlines from potential flow around an ellipsoid the size of the shoe, drawn as thin tubes with pulses running along them.
8. 21-24s: An Infrared field, the white Swoosh and "AIR MAX 90", the shoe turning to its side profile.

Every fact must come from Nike's own pages. Keep every headline and the shoe inside x 90-960 and y 250-1480 (only macros may leave on purpose, with no text over them).

Audio: an original track made in code (numpy): new jack swing in F minor at 80 BPM, so one bar is one 3-second block (gated-reverb snare, swung 16th hats, FM slap bass, FM brass stabs, an orchestra hit at the re-hook). Inside the Air unit the music goes low-passed, as if heard from inside. One sound per event, all rubber and air: a thud with a "pfff" of pressure on the drop, air rising on the dive, a bloop and glass inside, a warm buzz that sweeps with the color, a balloon stretch for AIR, three steps, wind in the tunnel, one tling on the Swoosh. Narration with Kokoro TTS, voice af_heart: "Visible Air." / "Since nineteen eighty-seven, you can see it." / "Pure, pressurized nitrogen." / "Infrared. The original colorway." / "This is Air." / "Every step, compressed and returned." / "Designed by Tinker Hatfield in nineteen ninety." / "Air Max Ninety." Check every line with Whisper. Master to -14 LUFS with a true peak at or below -1.5 dB.

Process: export a strip of stills every 1.5 seconds (including frame 0) with the safe zone marked. Wait for my approval before the final render.

What came back: one Remotion composition with the shoe loaded once, a camera path per block, the squash and the recolor as one shader patch shared by every material, the glass world, the inflated letters and the wind tunnel, with music, SFX and narration on the timeline.

05 · What Claude built

Everything is Air.

SQUASH

A soft sole in a shader

One vertex patch, shared by every material, compresses the midsole in the heel and lets it spring back, so the real model behaves like it is full of air.

COLOR

Violet to Infrared, live

The model's violet accents are found by hue in the fragment shader and turned Infrared behind a sheet of light that crosses the shoe.

BALLOONS

Letters full of air

Each letter's height comes from its distance to the edge, with a round profile, mirrored front and back and cut by the glyph. Inflating is a spring on its depth.

AIRFLOW

A real wind tunnel

Streamlines from the potential flow around an ellipsoid the size of the shoe, drawn as thin tubes with pulses of light running along them.

key code · GLSL / TypeScript
// the squash: one vertex patch for every material (shoe space)
float c = uSq * smoothstep(-0.5, 0.9, p.x);      // more in the heel
float y = p.y < 0.42 ? p.y * (1.0 - c) : p.y - 0.42 * c;

// the color: violet turns Infrared behind the moving front
float violet = smoothstep(0.13, 0.05, abs(hsv.x - 0.70));
rgb = mix(rgb, infra * (0.35 + 0.95 * hsv.z), violet * done);

// the airflow: potential flow around a sphere, in ellipsoid space
v = vec3(1.0 + 1.0 / (2.0 * r3), 0, 0) - 1.5 * q.x * q / r5;

08 · Watch out

What to check before you render.

Models that load after the frame

A model that arrives through fetch needs the 3D canvas advanced once before you continue the render, or the still comes out without the shoe.

Beveled is not inflated

An extruded bevel looks carved. Real balloons need a height that grows from the outline, and smooth normals: recomputing normals on extruded geometry flattens it.

Rings on smooth shapes

A distance field in whole-pixel steps shows up as rings at full size. Blur it, then force zero outside the shape.

The camera inside the model

A dive into a small opening ends inside the geometry if the last position is measured from the center. Measure from the surface.

Floors with a horizon

A big flat floor draws a hard line against the background. Fade it out with a radial alpha map.

Words the voice spells

"Comment AIR" in caps was read letter by letter. Write it in lowercase and check it with Whisper.

09 · Ship it

From render to post.

The render is not the finish line. These four steps decide how the piece looks once Instagram compresses it.

Step 01

Export at a real bitrate.

Deep red gradients band easily when compressed. Render at 14 Mbps.

Step 02

Check the loudness.

Aim for −14 LUFS with a true peak at or below −1.5 dB. The command limits the audio before encoding.

Step 03

Make the cover.

Use the most striking frame (here, the shoe in front of the inflated AIR). The profile grid crops covers to 3:4, so keep the hero between y 240 and 1680.

Step 04

Caption with the credits.

"Spec ad · not commissioned by [brand]", the trademark notice and the CC BY credit for the model.
