01 · The idea

Loud, silent, then the song.

The AirPods Pro promise is a before and after: the world is loud, then it isn't. So the spot is built as that contrast, three seconds at a time: a storm of noise, a wave that cancels it, a moment of real silence, and then the music moving everything on screen.

It is a spec piece built by the Lab with a real, licensed 3D model. One element carries the whole ad: a field of lines under the product that is noise, then flat, then the song's real spectrum, then a word.

00:01 · master prompt · claude code
Build a 27-second vertical spec ad for AirPods Pro called "Silence." in Remotion (React) with three.js through @remotion/three, 1080x1920 at 30 fps. The idea: the world is loud, Active Noise Cancellation makes it silent, and then the music moves everything. Use a real 3D product, never one built from boxes.

Model: load the CC BY 4.0 glTF "AirPods Pro" by Jed Falcone with GLTFLoader. Normalize it so the case is 6.06 units wide (1 unit = 1 cm; the real case is 60.6 mm), the LED facing +Z and the case centered on the origin. The lid is its own node: re-parent it under a hinge group at the back top edge of the case and open it with rotation.x = -1.83 (105 degrees) on a spring. The two earbuds come merged in one mesh per material: split the triangles left and right of the center into two groups, each with its pivot at its own center, so they can fly out and back. Glossy white plastic (MeshPhysicalMaterial, roughness 0.26, clearcoat 0.7), slightly rougher ear tips, and the LED as an emissive material that lights when the lid opens and closes.

The thread of the whole ad is ONE field of lines under the product: 140 lines across, each a thin ribbon with an opaque fill below it in the background color, so lines in front hide the ones behind (the "Unknown Pleasures" look). The field goes through four states: jagged red noise, flat after the anti-noise wave, the real spectrum of the song as a waterfall (each line further back shows an older frame), and finally the word LISTEN raised in the lines.

Music first: compose the track in code (numpy) with separate stems, and write a JSON with, for every frame, the energy of each stem and 40 log-spaced bands of the mix. The scene reads that JSON, so every visual reacts to the real song.

Scenes, one idea per 3 seconds:
1. 0-3s: "The world is loud." The closed case floats in a storm of 2,400 instanced shards (thin octahedra in coral and orange) swirling around it, above the jagged red field. Something moves from frame 0.
2. 3-6s: "Noise, cancelled. Up to 2x more Active Noise Cancellation than AirPods Pro 2.*" The lid opens at 3.4 s and an anti-noise ring of light expands across the field. Behind the ring the field goes flat, and every shard freezes the moment the wave reaches it (its local time is min(t, the time the wave hits it)). Under the headline, a small panel draws three waves: Noise, Anti-noise (the same wave inverted) and You hear (a flat line).
3. 6-9s: "Silence." Bullet time: the camera orbits through the frozen shards and everything cools to blue.
4. 9-12s: "Now, listen." The music drops on a log drum: the shards fly out and vanish, the field becomes the live spectrum, and the earbuds rise out of the case.
5. 12-15s: Re-hook, no headline: LIS / TEN in two rows, raised in a tight field of lines seen from above. In this block the ribbons lie flat, white inside the letters and dim outside.
6. 15-18s: "Spatial Audio. Personalized, with dynamic head tracking." One glowing sphere per instrument orbits the earbuds, its size following that stem's energy. The camera turns 90 degrees and the spheres stay where they are; the audio pans each stem with the same angle (sin of sphere angle minus camera yaw), so the sound really stays put.
7. 18-21s: "Heart rate sensing. Built into the earbuds." Macro of one earbud on deep wine red; a red glow pulses on every heartbeat and an ECG line draws itself behind it.
8. 21-24s: "Up to 8 hours. Of listening with ANC, on a single charge.*" The earbuds fly back into the case along glowing magnetic field lines (dipole curves, r = L sin^2 theta), snap in one after the other, and the lid closes.
9. 24-27s: A light packshot of the closed case. "AirPods Pro" written in text (Apple does not allow its logo for third parties), "Spec ad · not commissioned by Apple" and "*Results based on Apple testing."

Audio: an original amapiano track in G minor at 120 BPM made in code (log drum with a fast pitch drop, 16th shakers, rim, jazzy minor-9 piano stabs, an airy pad), entering at 9 s. Before it: a city-and-jet roar that the anti-noise wave shuts down with a closing low-pass, then near silence. One sound per event: a soft plastic clack when the lid opens, a glassy rise for the earbuds, the kick turning into a heartbeat in block 7, two magnetic snaps, the lid closing, and one soft two-note tone of our own at the end (never Apple's sounds). 05 · What Claude built

One field, four states.

FREEZE

Noise that stops in mid-air

Each of the 2,400 shards has its own clock that stops when the anti-noise ring reaches it, so the storm freezes from the case outward and the camera can orbit through it.

SPECTRUM

The real song, drawn

The music is composed first and analyzed frame by frame. Each line of the field shows the spectrum of an older frame, so the song scrolls away from you like a waterfall.

SPACE

Sound that stays put

One sphere per instrument, sized by its energy. The camera turns 90 degrees, and the audio pans each instrument with the same formula, so what you hear matches where you see it.

MAGNETS

Back in the case

The earbuds fly home along dipole field lines with light running through them, and each one lands on its own snap.

key code · TypeScript
// freeze: each shard stops the moment the anti-noise wave reaches it
const tHit = T0 + (T1 - T0) * Math.min(1, dist / R_FRONT);
const tau = Math.min(t, tHit);           // local time of this shard

// the song: one line per past frame, height from the real spectrum
h = (0.4 + 4.4 * spec[frame - i * lag][band]) * taper;

// spatial: the sound stays put while the camera turns
pan = Math.sin(orbAngle - camYaw(t));    // same formula in the audio

07 · Decisions

Where the taste is.

01

A real model or nothing

No case built from boxes. One licensed model with its lid on a hinge, and its credit.

02

One thread

The same field of lines runs the whole ad. It changes state; it never changes subject.

03

Silence you can see

Three seconds with almost no sound and nothing moving but the camera. The drop only works because of them.

04

No logo, on purpose

Apple's rules don't allow it, so the end card is the product and its name in text. It looks cleaner anyway.

Facts from the source

Every number is Apple's own, with "up to" and "based on Apple testing". The hearing feature is "authorized" by the FDA, not "approved", so we left it out rather than get it wrong.

08 · Watch out

What to check before you render.

Words in a vertical frame

A six-letter word laid on a wide field is wider than a 9:16 frame. Split it into two rows and size it to the safe zone.

Ribbons seen from above

Vertical ribbons disappear when the camera looks straight down. For the word, lay them flat and color the letters.

Instanced storms near the lens

A random swirl will eventually put a shard in front of the camera. Keep the storm's radius smaller than the camera's distance.

Noise that buries the music

Broadband noise is far louder than a song at the same peak. Mix the roar well below the music, or the drop sounds small.

Updating instances in Remotion

Write instanced matrices inside useFrame, not in a useEffect, or each frame shows the previous frame's positions.

Copied templates

When an edit is copied from a previous ad, check which frames of the new ad each clip points to: ours first showed the wrong block.

09 · Ship it

From render to post.

The render is not the finish line. These four steps decide how the piece looks once Instagram compresses it.

Step 01

Export at a real bitrate.

Thin lines and dark gradients band and smear when compressed. Render at 14 Mbps.

Step 02

Check the loudness.

Aim for −14 LUFS with a true peak at or below −1.5 dB. The command limits the audio before encoding.

Step 03

Make the cover.

Use the cleanest striking frame (here, the earbuds rising over the open case). The profile grid crops covers to 3:4, so keep the hero between y 240 and 1680.

Step 04

Caption with the credits.

"Spec ad · not commissioned by [brand]", the trademark notice and the CC BY credit for the model.
