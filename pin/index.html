01 · The idea

From the whole planet to one door.

Only a map app can go from the planet to a front door in one search. That is the whole spot: type, drop the pin, fall into the city, pick the fastest way and drive there. Each block shows one real feature: search, the place card, route options with time, distance and tolls, turn-by-turn navigation and arrival.

05 · What Claude built

Real data, rebuilt in code.

TERRAIN

The bay's real hills

Elevation tiles become a 640×640 mesh with gentle exaggeration, coloured by height and lit by a low sun from the west.

CITY

Streets as light

Every major road of the bay is a warm line on the terrain; downtown adds every street. The buildings are real footprints and heights with windows drawn in the shader.

ROUTES

A race with a winner

The three routes draw at speeds proportional to their times, so the fastest one finishes first and the numbers count up as each lands.

PIN

The logo, solid

The official icon outline extruded with a bevel, glossy and opaque, its four colours as a conic gradient around the hole. It only appears once a place is chosen.

07 · Decisions

Where the taste is.

01

Faithful UI, real contrast

Copying the whole app screen 1:1 was flat; dropping the UI lost the brand. The answer was both: the real interface from the screenshots, over a world with light and depth.

02

Real data over drawn maps

The streets, coastline, buildings, hills and routes are the real ones. It is what makes the bay recognisable at a glance.

03

The cut hides in the clouds

The opening clip and the 3D bay meet on the white of the clouds, so the dive reads as one camera.

04

The pin means a place

A pin floating next to the planet marks nothing. It drops only when the search has a destination, and the logo lives in the search bar from frame 0.

08 · Watch out

What to check before you render.

The coastline direction

OSM coastlines keep land on the left. Merging the ways can flip some of them and turn the sea into land: classify each face with the original way.

One-way bridges

A route forced through a bridge can pick the lane going the other way and add 15 miles. Use the westbound lanes.

The canvas does not repaint itself

In Remotion a three.js canvas only repaints when the frame changes. After a texture or data loads, advance the canvas once before releasing the render.

Memory

Real city data is heavy. If a long render dies near the end, render it in halves with fewer parallel tabs and join them.
