01 · The idea

One drop, every answer.

Most AI ads show a chat window. Here the product is a material: one drop of liquid chrome that takes the shape of whatever you ask. It forms the name, splits into ideas, frames a picture, vibrates with a voice and rains down as the number of people who use it. The viewer follows one substance, so six features read as one story.

It is a spec piece built by the Lab. OpenAI's brand guidelines don't allow its logo without permission, so the name only appears as a word, formed by the metal itself.

05 · What Claude built

A film that is one shader.

STRUCTURE

Six scenes, one distance field

The shader knows the time and the scene number. Each scene adds its shapes to the pool's distance field, so a drop, five spheres, a frame and an orb are all the same liquid.

THE NAME

Text as a distance field

The word is drawn once on a canvas, turned into an exact distance field and uploaded as a texture. The shader extrudes it with a rounded profile so the letters look poured, not typed.

MUSIC

Composed in code

A cinematic score in D minor written for this spot only: drones that breathe, tuned bells on each liquid event and two risers before the big moments.

VOICE

Kokoro TTS

Eight short lines in the Heart voice. The music ducks under the voice and the mix sits at −14 LUFS with a true peak at −2 dB.

key code · glsl
// the glue of every liquid shape: a smooth minimum
float smin(float a, float b, float k) {
  float h = clamp(0.5 + 0.5 * (b - a) / k, 0.0, 1.0);
  return mix(b, a, h) - k * h * (1.0 - h);
}
// the word: 2D distance from a texture, extruded with a pillowy profile
float d2 = (texture2D(uTex, uv).r - 0.5) * 2.0 * 40.0 * PX;
float h  = 0.012 + 0.07 * sqrt(R*R - (R - clamp(-d2, 0.0, R)) * (R - clamp(-d2, 0.0, R))) / R;
float word = -smin(-d2, -(abs(q.z) - h), 0.018);
LEVEL UP

Change the material, keep the method

Swap chrome for glass, honey or ink by changing only the shading at the end of the shader. The shapes, the timing and the camera stay the same.

Where the taste is.

The chrome name comes out of a splash at the start and again at the end, so the spot feels like one loop.

Every scene change is the liquid changing shape. The camera eases between keyframes and never cuts.

05

Fast, but readable

06

Stills before render

A shader render takes minutes; a still takes seconds. Every fix was found on a contact sheet first.

08 · Watch out

What to check before you render.

Dark letters

Faces that look straight at the camera reflect what is behind it, which in a studio is usually black. Add a soft light behind the camera that only the word reflects.

Noisy edges

An 8-bit distance texture makes speckles if you compute normals with a tiny step. Use a bigger step (about 0.006) on the letters and soften the edge with a smooth maximum.

Stills without letters

In a Remotion render the three.js loop is paused. After the texture loads, call advance(performance.now()) from useThree before continueRender, or some frames render without the word.

Verify every number

The first draft said "more than 1.2 billion, Oct 2026". The source says 1.2 billion, announced at DevDay on Sep 29, 2026. Put the source on screen and in the caption.

Small details near the edge

Decorations count too: the four voice dots sat under the safe zone until the stills showed it.

09 · Ship it

From render to post.

The render is not the finish line. These four steps decide how the piece looks once Instagram compresses it.

Step 01

Export at a real bitrate.

Renderers often come out at 5–6 Mbps on flat designs, and Instagram compresses again. Re-encode at a constant 14 Mbps with the command below.

Step 02

Check the loudness.

Aim for −14 LUFS with a true peak at or below −1.5 dB. The AAC encoder can push peaks up, so the command limits the audio before encoding.

Step 03

Make the cover.

Use the frame that shows the product best. The profile grid crops covers to 3:4 (the middle 1080×1440), so keep the headline and the hero between y 240 and 1680.

Step 04

Caption with the disclaimer.

A spec ad says so. End the caption with "Spec ad · not commissioned by [brand]. Inspired by [brand]."

export · ffmpeg
ffmpeg -i render.mp4 -c:v libx264 -preset slow -b:v 14M -minrate 14M -maxrate 14M -bufsize 14M -x264-params nal-hrd=cbr -pix_fmt yuv420p -af "aresample=192000,alimiter=limit=0.78:level=false,aresample=48000" -c:a aac -b:a 320k -movflags +faststart final.mp4
